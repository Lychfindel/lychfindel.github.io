I am a Research Engineer and Staff Scientist at IRCCS San Camillo Hospital in Venice.

My main research interest is the application of Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) technologies for rehabilitation and assistive purposes. As Staff Scientist, I also contribute to a broader range of projects, supporting signal analysis, experiment design, and the engineering side of research.

I also strongly support Open Science, Open Source, Open Data, and Openness in general.
